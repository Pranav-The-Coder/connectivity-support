About the app

Connectivity,
made visible.

Connectivity is an iOS app for crowdsourcing and visualizing internet connection quality. Speed tests combine download and upload measurements into a connection score from 0 to 100.

Use the live map to view color-graded circles from tested locations, preview routes between map points, configure test and refresh intervals, or browse in View Only Mode. Map information can be refreshed manually or automatically.

Frequently asked questions

A few useful things to know about how Connectivity works.

What is Connectivity?

Connectivity is an iOS application that helps users view and understand connection quality using testing data and a map.

What does the map show?

The map displays connection-quality information from tested locations.

How is the connection score calculated?

The app scores connection quality from 0 to 100 using both download and upload speed. The score uses a weighted, logarithmic blend, so it is a guide to the tested connection rather than a guarantee of future performance.

Why does my connection score change?

Connection quality can vary based on your location, network conditions, signal conditions, congestion, and other factors.

Why is my area showing weak connectivity?

Connection conditions can vary by location and time. Map data may not perfectly represent your current connection.

What permissions and data does the app use?

Location access may be used to tag speed-test samples and display them on the map, including while the app is active in the background when permitted. Speed tests may collect download and upload speeds, connection type, and radio technology. See the Privacy Policy for the project’s current details.

Can I use the app without running tests?

Yes. Connectivity includes a view-only mode for browsing map information without necessarily running tests yourself.

Why is the map not updating?

Check your internet connection, make sure the app has the required permissions, try manually refreshing the map, and close and reopen the app. If the issue continues, contact support.

How do I report a bug or request help?

Use the Contact Support section below to email the development team. Include as much detail as possible so the issue can be understood.

Something not working?

Troubleshooting

Try the relevant steps below. If the issue continues, we are here to help.

01

Map is not loading

  • Check your internet connection.
  • Make sure the app is updated.
  • Try refreshing the map or restarting the app.
  • Contact support if the issue continues.
02

Location information appears incorrect

  • Check that location permissions are enabled.
  • Remember that GPS accuracy can vary with environmental and device conditions.
  • Try again from a location with a clearer signal.
03

Speed test does not work

  • Check your internet connectivity and required permissions.
  • Try again later or restart the app.
  • Contact support if the problem continues.
04

App behaves unexpectedly

  • Close and reopen the app.
  • Make sure iOS and the app are updated.
  • Contact support with details of what happened.

Privacy &
Permissions.
