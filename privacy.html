Connectivity

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Connectivity (“the app”) is developed for the Congressional App Challenge.

Information We Collect

  • Location data: used to tag speed-test samples and display signal strength on the map. Location may be collected in the background while the app is active, based on your permission settings.
  • Account information: a username and password are required to create an account. Passwords are stored using salted cryptographic hashing and are never stored or transmitted in plain text.
  • Network performance data: download/upload speeds, connection type (WiFi/Cellular), and radio technology are collected during speed tests to build the connectivity map.

How We Use Your Information

  • Location and speed data are used solely to power the crowdsourced signal map shown to all users.
  • Your username may be publicly associated with your contributed data, such as on the leaderboard.
  • We do not sell your data to third parties.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.

Your Choices

  • You can enable View Only Mode in Settings to browse the map without contributing your own data.
  • You can disable location permissions at any time through iOS Settings.

Data Storage

Data is stored on our servers and used only to power the features described above.
